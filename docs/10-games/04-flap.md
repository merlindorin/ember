# Flap

![Flap: an orange bird between two pairs of pipes under a night sky, beside its score and best](flap.png "ember · flap")

Space, Up or a click flaps; clouds and hills scroll slower than the pipes, and the physics steps at 120 Hz whatever the frame rate.

[Play in your browser](https://ember.vanyda.cloud/ember/live/index.html?wasm=games-flap.wasm.gz) · [The code](https://github.com/merlindorin/ember/tree/main/games/flap) · `go run ./games/flap`
