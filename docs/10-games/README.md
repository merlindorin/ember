# Games

Two small, whole games made with ember: the rules in plain Go, the board a 2D entity, the HUD and menus ui, the music and sounds made in code.

## Tetris

![Tetris: a board of falling pieces beside its score, next piece, lines and level](tetris.png "ember · tetris")

Line clears flash and spark; Escape opens the menu, with the leaderboard and the sound.

[Play in your browser](https://ember.vanyda.cloud/ember/live/index.html?wasm=games-tetris.wasm.gz) · [The code](https://github.com/merlindorin/ember/tree/main/games/tetris) · `go run ./games/tetris`

## 2048

![2048: a board of numbered tiles beside its score and best tile](2048.png "ember · 2048")

Tiles slide and merges pop; the same menu, leaderboard and sound as Tetris.

[Play in your browser](https://ember.vanyda.cloud/ember/live/index.html?wasm=games-2048.wasm.gz) · [The code](https://github.com/merlindorin/ember/tree/main/games/2048) · `go run ./games/2048`
