# 2048

![2048: a board of numbered tiles beside its score and best tile](2048.png "ember · 2048")

Tiles slide with the arrows, the pad's cross or its stick, and merges pop; the same menu, leaderboard and sound as Tetris.

[Play in your browser](https://ember.vanyda.cloud/ember/live/index.html?wasm=games-2048.wasm.gz) · [The code](https://github.com/merlindorin/ember/tree/main/games/2048) · `go run ./games/2048`
